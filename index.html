Team Spotlights

One player from each team and what they've done so far this season.

Plymouth State Panthers

Panthers · Home team

Player to watch

TJ Taveras

DL · Sr. · Rochester, N.H.

19 tackles · 6.0 TFL · 3.5 sacks (MASCAC best)

Taveras has been the Panthers' tone-setter on defense, leading the MASCAC with 3.5 sacks while tying for second in the conference with 6.0 tackles for loss. The senior defensive lineman posted a season-high 11 tackles and 3.5 TFL in Plymouth State's 14–0 Homecoming shutout of Dean, and he has added six quarterback hurries and a forced fumble.

New England College Pilgrims

Pilgrims

Player to watch

Ethan Cenesca

LB

63 tackles · 11.5 TFL · 2 INT · 2 FF

Cenesca has been a bright spot for the Pilgrims, racking up 63 total tackles (34 solo) to go with 11.5 tackles for loss, 2.0 sacks, two interceptions and two forced fumbles in five games. He was at his best against Plymouth State on Sept. 5, when he recorded 13 tackles and 4.5 TFL in the season opener.

Worcester State Lancers

Lancers

Player to watch

M.J. Bakaysa

QB

1,212 pass yds (MASCAC best) · 11 TD

Bakaysa directs one of the MASCAC's most productive passing attacks, leading the conference with 1,212 passing yards and 11 touchdowns while averaging 242.4 yards per game. The Lancers' quarterback threw for 191 yards and two scores in Worcester State's 28–6 win over Plymouth State on Sept. 12.

Bridgewater State Bears

Bears

Player to watch

Jayden Barber

QB

71.8% completions · 184.0 rating · 7 TD

Barber has been the MASCAC's most efficient quarterback, topping the conference in completion percentage (71.8%) and pass efficiency rating (184.0). He has thrown for 697 yards and seven touchdowns and averages 7.8 yards per carry, and he led the Bears to a 35–7 win at Plymouth State on Sept. 19.

Dean College Bulldogs

Bulldogs

Player to watch

Chris Donohue

QB

102 completions (MASCAC best) · 716 yds · 179.0 ypg

Donohue keeps the Bulldogs' offense moving, leading the MASCAC with 102 completions on 156 attempts (65.4%) for 716 yards and three touchdowns. Against Plymouth State on Oct. 3 he completed 23 of 28 passes and also ran 26 times, accounting for most of Dean's offense in a 14–0 loss.

4 rush TD · 131 yds · 6.2 avg

Schwartz has been the Buccaneers' finisher in the red zone, scoring a team-high four rushing touchdowns in just three games. The running back has turned 21 carries into 131 yards (6.2 per attempt) with a long run of 23 yards.
